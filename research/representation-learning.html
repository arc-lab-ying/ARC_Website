Representation Learning for the Built Environment

Overview

We investigate how engineering knowledge can be learned directly from the BIM models and other digital data generated across the built environment lifecycle. Our research develops representation learning methods that capture patterns, relationships, and latent knowledge in these data, enabling more generalizable and data-driven engineering intelligence.
