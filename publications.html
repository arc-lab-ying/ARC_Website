Generating second-level space boundaries from large-scale IFC-compliant building information models using multiple geometry representationsGitHub

Published in Automation in Construction, 2021 Journal

This paper presents an approach for generating second-level space boundaries from IFC building models using multiple geometric representations, supporting more efficient BIM-to-BEM information extraction for large-scale and complex buildings.

Recommended citation: Ying, H., & Lee, S. (2021). Generating second-level space boundaries from large-scale IFC-compliant building information models using multiple geometry representations. Automation in Construction, 126, 103659. doi: 10.1016/j.autcon.2021.103659.

A rule-based system to automatically validate IFC second-level space boundaries for building energy analysis

Published in Automation in Construction, 2021 Journal

This paper develops an automated rule-based system for checking the syntactic, semantic, geometric, and consistency quality of IFC second-level space boundaries before their use in building energy analysis.

Recommended citation: Ying, H., & Lee, S. (2021). A rule-based system to automatically validate IFC second-level space boundaries for building energy analysis. Automation in Construction, 127, 103724. doi: 10.1016/j.autcon.2021.103724.

An algorithm to facet curved walls in IFC BIM for building energy analysis

Published in Automation in Construction, 2019 Journal

This paper proposes an algorithm for automatically faceting curved walls in IFC building models so that curved building elements can be transformed for building energy modeling workflows.

Recommended citation: Ying, H., & Lee, S. (2019). An algorithm to facet curved walls in IFC BIM for building energy analysis. Automation in Construction, 103, 80-103. doi: 10.1016/j.autcon.2019.03.004.

A Mask R-CNN based approach to automatically construct as-is IFC BIM objects from digital images

Published in Proceedings of the 36th ISARC, 2019 Conference

This paper adapts Mask R-CNN to recognize and segment building objects from digital images, then geometrically fits the segmented objects to construct as-is IFC BIM objects.

Recommended citation: Ying, H., & Lee, S. (2019). A Mask R-CNN based approach to automatically construct as-is IFC BIM objects from digital images. Proceedings of the 36th International Symposium on Automation and Robotics in Construction, 764-771. doi: 10.22260/ISARC2019/0103.
